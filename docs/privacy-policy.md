# Privacy Policy

**Goodwookie Productions LLC**
Effective date: September 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Goodwookie Productions LLC ("Goodwookie", "we", "us") collects through goodwookie.com and the Design Your Pet app, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have a question that isn't answered here, email us at info@goodwookie.com.

## The short version

- You upload a photo of your pet. We use it to create your artwork, and for nothing else.
- Your photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to create the artwork and to run safety checks.
- If you order, we share your name, shipping address and contact details with our print partner, Printful, so they can make and ship your order.
- Payments are handled by our checkout and payment providers. We never see or store your full card number.
- We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.
- Designs you don't order are deleted automatically after 7 days.

## What we collect

**Pet photos.** You upload the photo to create a design. It's stored in our website's media storage while we make your artwork.

**Pet names or short text.** The Stamp and Poster styles can include up to 18 characters of text, such as your pet's name.

**The artwork we create.** Each design is saved in two versions: a print-quality file used only to make your order, and a watermarked preview that you see on screen.

**A device identifier.** Your browser or app stores a random ID. It isn't linked to your name. We use it to show you your recent designs under My Designs and to apply the daily design limits. If you clear your browser data or reinstall the app, you get a new ID and your previous designs will no longer appear.

**Order information.** When you buy, you give us your name, shipping address, email address and, optionally, a phone number, together with what you ordered.

**Payment information.** Payments are processed by our checkout provider (Wix) and payment providers, including credit and debit card processors, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. They handle your payment details under their own privacy policies. We receive confirmation that you paid, not your full card number.

**Website data.** Like most websites, goodwookie.com is hosted on Wix and uses cookies and similar technology to operate the site, keep your cart, and measure basic site traffic. See the Wix cookie information linked at the bottom of our site for details.

## How we use it

- **To create your artwork.** Your photo and any text you enter are sent to Google's Gemini API, which draws the design.
- **To keep the service safe.** Before any artwork is made, the photo is automatically checked for inappropriate content and to confirm there's an animal in it. Text you enter is checked the same way. Finished designs get a further automatic check. Designs that fail are not printed. A design flagged by the check may be reviewed by us before it's printed.
- **To show you product previews.** Your watermarked preview is sent to Printful to create a picture of how it will look on the product.
- **To make and deliver your order.** Your print-quality design, name, shipping address and contact details are sent to Printful, which prints and ships your order.
- **To run the business.** This covers answering your questions, handling returns and problems, preventing abuse (for example, the daily design limits), and keeping the records the law requires.

We do not use your photos or designs for marketing, and we do not use them to train AI models.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers who help us run Design Your Pet:

| Provider | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Google (Gemini API)** | Your pet photo, text you enter, and the generated artwork | To create the artwork and run safety checks |
| **Printful** | Your design and order and shipping details | To print and ship your order and create product previews |
| **Wix** | All of the above, as our website and data host | To host the site, store designs, and run the store and checkout |
| **Payment providers** (card processors, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay) | Your payment details | To take payment |

Google's terms for its paid API services say that it does not use the content sent to it to improve its products. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

## How long we keep it

- **Photos that fail the safety check,** or that have no animal in them, are deleted permanently straight away.
- **Designs you don't order,** and the photos they came from, are deleted automatically after 7 days.
- **Designs you order,** and your order details, are kept as part of our business and tax records, and so we can deal with reprints or problems with your order.
- **My Designs** shows designs from the last 7 days.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:
- tell you what personal information we hold about you;
- correct it;
- delete your photos, designs or order information, except for records the law requires us to keep.

To make a request, email info@goodwookie.com. Include your order number if you have one. Designs you haven't ordered aren't linked to your name, so we may ask for the approximate date and style to find them.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law. We will honor them as the law requires.

## Photos of people

Design Your Pet draws animals only. If a photo includes people, they are left out of the artwork. Please upload only photos that you have the right to use.

## Children

Design Your Pet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

We use the security measures built into our website and service providers. The print-quality files are never sent to your browser; you only ever see the watermarked preview. No online service can be perfectly secure, however.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we'll also post a notice on the site.
